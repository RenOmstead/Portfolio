ABOUT ME

I like figuring out how things could work better.

My work crosses marketing, frontend development, research, analytics, and business operations.

I am most interested in the point where those areas overlap — where a better system, interface, process, or strategy can make the work itself easier.

HOW I GOT HERE

I did not stay inside one lane.

I started with a business and marketing background, but the more I worked with websites, campaigns, analytics, CRM systems, and digital tools, the more interested I became in how those systems were built.

That curiosity led me further into frontend development, databases, APIs, product thinking, and internal business systems.

Today, I am comfortable moving between strategy and execution — researching an opportunity, planning a campaign, designing a workflow, or building the interface that supports it.

I tend to gravitate toward problems that are messy, cross-functional, or difficult to fit neatly into one department.
